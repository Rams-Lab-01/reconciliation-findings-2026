Task 6 — Verification of the AI briefing memo

Subject of review: an internal briefing memo to principals on UAE finance-ops rules. This page records what is correct, what is wrong, what is misleading by omission, and which source backs each finding. All sources are official UAE government or FTA domains.
Summary. Of the 12 statements in the memo, 6 are wrong, 4 are correct, and 2 are correct but incomplete in a way that matters for a DMCC family office. The most consequential wrong statement is #6 (CT return deadline is 9 months, not 12). The most consequential omission is the Qualifying Free Zone Person regime in #5, which reduces CT on Qualifying Income from 9% to 0% up to AED 3,015,000.

2 — Two correct-but-incomplete statements that matter for a DMCC family office

Statement #5 (corporate tax rate). A DMCC family office that qualifies as a Qualifying Free Zone Person (QFZP) under Cabinet Decision No. 55/2023 pays 0% on Qualifying Income up to AED 3,015,000 (not AED 375,000), and 9% only on non-Qualifying Income — missing this regime could cost the office hundreds of thousands of dirhams annually on income that should be zero-rated.
Statement #11 (natural person threshold). Once above AED 1M, natural-person business income still benefits from the AED 375,000 0% threshold before the 9% marginal rate applies, and personal investment income and certain real-estate income can be excluded — important for principals and family beneficiaries who hold assets individually.

3 — Single wrong statement that would have cost real money or trouble first

Statement #6 (corporate tax return due within 12 months of financial year end — actual deadline is 9 months). Missing the CT filing deadline triggers a late-filing penalty of AED 500/month plus 1%/month interest on any unpaid tax, and for a profitable DMCC family office the penalty runs into tens of thousands of dirhams within the first quarter past the missed deadline — exactly the operational error this kind of internal briefing is supposed to prevent.
