Principal finding. The statement shows cheque 0470 for AED 93,750.00 clearing on 19 Aug 2026 as one clean debit. The cheque is dated 01 Sep 2026, so it was paid thirteen days before the date written on its face. In UAE clearing practice a postdated cheque presented early is normally returned, and a return never appears as a single tidy line. It appears as a debit, then a reversing credit, then a narration carrying the return reason. This statement shows no reversal and no reason narration. That does not by itself prove wrongdoing, but it is the first item to put to the bank in writing, and the entity is meanwhile out of pocket by AED 93,750.00 almost two weeks early.

2. How a returned cheque appears on a UAE statement, and why this one does not

A return is never a single clean line. The bank has to evidence why it declined to pay, so the entry always comes as a group. Below is the shape the statement would have taken had cheque 0470 been returned on 19 Aug, set against what is actually held.

How this is phrased if challenged. It is not asserted that a UAE bank can never pay an early presented cheque, because the law does not place an absolute bar on payment and banks retain discretion. The position is that system practice holds postdated cheques to their dates, that early presentations are routinely returned, and that payment ahead of the stated date is therefore an exception requiring documentary explanation. On charges, a fee is normally applied from the first return under published schedules, in the region of AED 100 to 350 on a business account, and waiver is a discretionary relationship matter rather than an automatic first time allowance. Separately, the Central Bank administrative regime applies to repeated returns, leading to chequebook withdrawal and account closure, which is a consequence of a different order from a handling fee.

6. Most serious control failure and the control to add

The failureAED 93,750.00 left the account thirteen days before the date on the cheque, in a form that carries none of the return markings such a presentation would normally produce, and neither record raised anything, because both carried the same entry and a reconciliation can only find differences.
The controlLodge every postdated cheque with the bank in advance through a positive pay instruction carrying number, payee, amount and due date, and hold it in a cheques issued but not yet due liability account rather than crediting cash, so that no instrument can be honoured or recorded as paid before the date written on its face.
