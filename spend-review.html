4. What this memo does and does not do

This is a spend review, not a financial audit and not a legal audit. Findings are based on the 74 transactions and card statements provided. The AED 12,460.00 VAT payment on 24 Aug is a balance-sheet settlement of a liability accrued in earlier periods and is excluded from operating spend. Items not provided — notably the full statement for CARD 0951 — remain untested.
