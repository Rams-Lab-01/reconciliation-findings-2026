4 — Detailed corrections

Round 1 — Statement 2 (VAT threshold)

What I said before"Mandatory threshold is AED 187,500. CORRECT."
What is trueMandatory threshold under Article 13 of Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017 is AED 375,000. AED 187,500 is the voluntary threshold under Cabinet Decision No. 52/2017 Art 15.
How the audit caught itThe audit cited Article 13 directly and flagged that I had accepted a secondary summary about a threshold reduction.
SourceFTA: Registration for VAT

Round 1 — Statement 8 (cheque partials) — final reversal in Round 2

What I said at Round 1"Banks do not make partial payments on cheques."
What is actually trueThe memo is right. Article 648(2) of Federal Decree-Law No. 50 of 2022 legally mandates drawee banks to make partial payment of available funds unless the holder refuses. Article 667 is the separate executive-instrument provision: a cheque bearing the drawee’s statement of non-payment is an executive document enforceable in whole or in part. The cheque is marked on the back and returned to the bearer; the bearer retains the right to recover the unpaid balance via the partially paid cheque.
How the reversal happenedCBUAE cheques FAQ + full text of Federal Decree-Law No. 50/2022 + commentary from UAE legal practitioners all confirmed mandatory partial payment. My Round 1 verdict was based on a misread.
SourceCBUAE Cheques FAQ; Federal Decree-Law No. 50/2022

Round 1 — Statement 12 (e-invoicing dates)

What I said before"Phase 1 mandatory from 1 July 2026 for revenue > AED 50M."
What is true1 July 2026 was the voluntary pilot launch. Mandatory Phase 1 begins 1 January 2027. ASP appointment deadline is 31 July 2026.

Round 2 — Statement 4 (late VAT registration penalty)

What I said before"Late VAT registration penalty is AED 20,000. CORRECT."
What is trueThe penalty is AED 10,000, reduced from AED 20,000 by Cabinet Decision No. 49 of 2021 effective 28 June 2021. The memo quotes the superseded figure.

Round 2.5 — Statement 4 (late VAT registration penalty)

What I said originally"The penalty for late VAT registration is AED 20,000. CORRECT."
What is trueThe penalty is AED 10,000. Cabinet Decision No. 49 of 2021 replaced the penalty tables annexed to Cabinet Decision No. 40 of 2017; Table 1, item 3 sets failure by a Taxable Person to submit a registration application within the specified timeframe at AED 10,000. The decision took effect 28 June 2021.

5 — Lessons for future regulatory verifications

1. When a secondary source gives a specific figure or date, cross-check against the primary statute or regulator before treating it as the right answer. The 187.5k-vs-375k and 20k-vs-10k errors both came from relying on secondary summaries.
2. When the memo makes a confident-sounding claim about a specific article or section ("the threshold is now 187.5k", "banks do not pay partials"), quote and verify the exact article number against the law. The 2022 Cabinet Decision 100 is real but amends voluntary registration, not mandatory.
3. Distinguish pilot / voluntary phases from mandatory phases. Phased rollouts (e.g. e-invoicing) have a confusing news cycle where "Phase 1 starting July" is repeated even when "Phase 1" means different things in different sources. The MoF press release is the canonical source.
4. Treat any confident assertion that contradicts a primary statute citation (Article 13, Article 53, Article 667) as a signal to dig further. The audit's value was exactly this — it forced me back to the articles.
5. When the memo says something that feels like a settled business norm ("banks do not pay partials") and the audit pushes back, re-read the statute. The cheque law is in fact explicit on partials; the assumption that it is not came from a stale reading.
