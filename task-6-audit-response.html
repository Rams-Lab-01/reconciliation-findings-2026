2 — Detailed corrections

Correction 1 — Statement 2: VAT registration threshold

What I said before"Mandatory threshold is AED 187,500 (reduced from AED 375,000 effective 1 April 2022). CORRECT."
What is actually trueThe mandatory threshold under Article 13 of Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017 is AED 375,000. AED 187,500 is the voluntary registration threshold under Article 17 / Cabinet Decision No. 52/2017 Art 15.
How the audit caught itThe audit cited Article 13 and flagged that I had accepted a false claim about a threshold reduction.
How I re-verifiedWeb search of FTA primary guidance — confirmed AED 375,000 mandatory, AED 187,500 voluntary. Multiple UAE tax-advisory sources concur.
SourceFTA: tax.gov.ae; Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017 Arts 13 and 17; Cabinet Decision No. 52 of 2017 Art 15.
Why the original error happenedI was probably pattern-matching a 2022-era news cycle that conflated the voluntary threshold with a mandatory reduction. Should have re-read Article 13 instead of relying on the secondary summary.

Correction 2 — Statement 8: Cheque partial payments

What I said before"Cheque bouncing can still be criminal … Banks do not make partial payments on cheques."
What is actually trueCheque bouncing can still be criminal — that part was right. The partial-payment part was wrong. Article 667 of Federal Decree-Law No. 50 of 2022 legally mandates the drawee bank to make partial payment from available funds unless the bearer refuses. The cheque is marked on the back, returned to the bearer, and the bearer can use the partially paid cheque as an executive instrument to recover the balance.
How the audit caught itThe audit cited Article 667 of the Commercial Transactions Law directly.
How I re-verifiedCBUAE cheques FAQ + the full text of Federal Decree-Law No. 50 of 2022 + commentary from UAE legal practitioners. All confirm mandatory partial-payment.
SourceCBUAE cheques FAQ; Federal Decree-Law No. 50 of 2022; Leaders Advocates
Why the original error happenedI assumed "bouncing" and "partial payment" were both undefined or informal bank practices, when in fact Article 667 codifies them. Should have searched the Central Bank FAQ before writing.

Correction 3 — Statement 12: E-invoicing rollout dates

What I said before"Phase 1 mandatory from 1 July 2026 for businesses with revenue > AED 50M."
What is actually true1 July 2026 was the voluntary pilot launch / Taxpayer Working Group onboarding. The mandatory Phase 1 begins 1 January 2027 for businesses with annual revenue ≥ AED 50M. ASP appointment deadline is 30/31 October 2026. Phase 2 (revenue < AED 50M) follows.
How the audit caught itThe audit distinguished "pilot" from "mandatory" and pointed at the 1 January 2027 effective date.
How I re-verifiedKPMG, Deloitte, MoF all confirm: 1 July 2026 = pilot/voluntary; 1 January 2027 = mandatory Phase 1 for revenue ≥ AED 50M.
SourceMoF; Deloitte; KPMG
Why the original error happenedEarlier secondary sources reported "Phase 1 from 1 July 2026" without distinguishing pilot from mandatory. Should have cross-checked the MoF primary release rather than repeating the secondary framing.

4 — Lessons for future regulatory verifications

1. When a secondary source gives a specific figure or date, cross-check against the primary statute or regulator before treating it as the right answer. The 187.5k-vs-375k error came from relying on a secondary summary.
2. When the memo makes a confident-sounding claim about a specific article or section ("the threshold is now 187.5k", "banks do not pay partials"), quote and verify the exact article number against the law. The 2022 Cabinet Decision 100 is real but amends voluntary registration, not mandatory.
3. Distinguish pilot / voluntary phases from mandatory phases. Phased rollouts (e.g. e-invoicing) have a confusing news cycle where "Phase 1 starting July" is repeated even when "Phase 1" means different things in different sources. The MoF press release is the canonical source.
4. Treat any confident assertion that contradicts a primary statute citation (Article 13, Article 617, Article 667) as a signal to dig further. The audit's value was exactly this — it forced me back to the articles.
