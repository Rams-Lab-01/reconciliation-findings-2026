What our VAT return must do about D3. We must self account for output VAT of AED 550.88 on the imported service under the reverse charge mechanism, declaring it in box 3 and recovering the same AED 550.88 in box 10, which is the input-side reverse-charge box on form VAT 201; box 9 is standard-rated expenses, so the net cash effect is nil. The AED 550.88 shown on the CloudMetrics invoice is not UAE VAT, is not recoverable as input tax, and must not be paid to the vendor. (FTA VAT Returns User Guide, Box 10: reverse-charge VAT declared in Boxes 3, 6 and 7 is recovered in Box 10. The Box 9 exclusion list states expressly that purchases subject to the reverse charge are recovered in Box 10, not Box 9.)

6. Standing process for any vendor bank detail change

Verify out of band Never use any contact detail contained in the request itself. Call back only on the number already held in the vendor master, to the named contact already on file, and record who was spoken to, on which number and at what time. If the master contact cannot be reached, the change does not proceed.
Document and dual approve Require a signed request on vendor letterhead together with a bank issued account certificate, then run the IBAN through a structural and bank code check before anything is keyed. Two people are required, one to enter the amendment and a different, more senior person to approve it, and neither may be the person who received the email.
Cool off and test pay Apply a mandatory waiting period during which no payment releases to newly amended details, then settle the first invoice after the change with a small verification amount confirmed by the vendor before the balance follows. Log every amendment with a full audit trail and retain the previous details rather than overwriting them.
