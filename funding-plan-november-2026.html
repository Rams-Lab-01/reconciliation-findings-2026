5. If the wire arrived one banking day late

The rule that breaks Cheque funds must be in the account by the banking day before presentation. Under the single-draw plan the money is due Fri 6 Nov for a Mon 9 Nov presentation, so one day late puts it in on the presentation day itself and the rule is breached with zero margin.
What actually happens Nothing bounces, because the opening 115,000.00 covers the 93,750.00 cheque unaided. But the account then sits at 21,250.00 against a 25,000.00 buffer, so the buffer rule breaks as well, and we would be relying on the bank's intraday posting order rather than on a control. Under the two-tranche plan the same one-day slip on tranche 2 lands it Tue 24 Nov, the salary file submission day, leaving no cushion at all on a WPS run.
The one change Request every funding tranche three banking days ahead of the binding deadline rather than two, so there is always one spare banking day. Concretely, move the request from Wed 4 Nov to Tue 3 Nov, which credits Thu 5 Nov and leaves Fri 6 Nov as free slack, and move tranche 2 from Thu 19 Nov to Wed 18 Nov.
